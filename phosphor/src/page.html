How to play

  • Jump: tap, click, or press Space. Hold to keep jumping. One jump lasts exactly one beat.
  • Pads launch you on contact. Rings give a second jump if you tap while touching them.
  • Gates change how you move. FLY: hold to climb, release to sink. ZIG: hold to rise, release to dive. G UP flips gravity. Number gates change speed.
  • Anything in the hot colour ends the run. So does hitting a wall.
  • Practice drops checkpoints so you can learn a level one section at a time.
  • Cores are three optional pickups per level, usually off the safe path.
  • Keys: P pause, R restart, Z / X add or remove a checkpoint.
